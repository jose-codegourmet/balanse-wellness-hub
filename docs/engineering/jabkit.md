# Jabkit convention

- Config: `apps/web/jabkit.config.json` and `apps/admin/jabkit.config.json`.
- `componentsDir`: `src/components/jabkit`
- `alias`: `@/components/jabkit`
- `registry`: `https://jabkit.joseadrianbuctuanon.dev` (override with `JABKIT_REGISTRY`)
- `formatter`: `biome`

Vendored Jabkit files are not hand-edited. Wrappers and product composition live in `src/components/balanse/`.

Browse installed names at `/dev/kit`. Tokens at `/dev/tokens`.
