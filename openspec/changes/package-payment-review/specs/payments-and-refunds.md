# Delta: Manual payments and refunds

Base: `openspec/specs/payments-and-refunds.md` (requirements 1–7). Merge on archive.

## MODIFIED Requirements

7. Paid package acquisition has a dedicated manual payment and review record. **The customer flow (method choice, GCash send/upload/review, Pay at Counter) reuses the booking payment components and copy structure, without any hold deadline. Staff review it on the Payments screen's Packages tab, inspect the receipt by short-lived audited signed URL, and approve, reject with a reason, or record cash.** Package credits activate only after staff approval; see [session bundles](session-bundles.md). Booking payment and package acquisition are separate records.
